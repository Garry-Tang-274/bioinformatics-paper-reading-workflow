# Contributing Guide

新增模板必须解决明确的阅读或审计问题，并保持中文段落后紧跟严格对应的英文段落。

New templates must solve a defined reading or audit problem and keep each Chinese paragraph immediately followed by its strictly corresponding English paragraph.

请勿提交论文全文、付费附件、未经许可的图表或包含个人隐私的数据。

Do not submit full papers, paywalled supplements, unauthorized figures, or data containing personal information.

示例应使用合成数据、公共领域内容或获得明确许可的材料，并注明来源与许可。

Examples should use synthetic data, public-domain content, or explicitly licensed material, with sources and licenses stated.
