# 组会汇报提纲
# Journal-Club Presentation Outline

## 第 1 页：问题与结论
## Slide 1: Problem and Conclusion

用一个研究问题、一张概念图和一句结论建立全局框架。

Establish the global frame with one research question, one conceptual diagram, and one conclusion.

## 第 2 页：数据与实验设计
## Slide 2: Data and Experimental Design

展示数据来源、样本量、划分方式、对照和潜在泄漏路径。

Show data sources, sample sizes, split design, controls, and potential leakage paths.

## 第 3 页：方法
## Slide 3: Method

只保留理解结果所需的模块，并用输入—处理—输出图替代大段文字。

Keep only the modules required to understand the results, and replace long text with an input-process-output diagram.

## 第 4 至 6 页：关键结果
## Slides 4–6: Key Results

每页只回答一个问题，标题直接写结论，并在页脚标注指标、样本量和统计信息。

Each slide should answer one question, state the conclusion in the title, and note metrics, sample sizes, and statistics in the footer.

## 最后一页：局限与下一步
## Final Slide: Limitations and Next Steps

列出最重要的两个局限、一个复现计划和一个对当前项目的可执行启发。

List the two most important limitations, one reproduction plan, and one actionable implication for the current project.
