# 论文精读报告模板
# Paper Reading Report Template

## 书目信息
## Bibliographic Information

论文题目、作者、期刊或预印本平台、年份、DOI、代码地址和数据地址。

Record the title, authors, journal or preprint platform, year, DOI, code URL, and data URL.

## 一句话结论
## One-Sentence Conclusion

用一句话说明论文做了什么、在什么条件下有效，以及最重要的限制。

In one sentence, state what the paper did, under what conditions it worked, and its most important limitation.

## 研究问题与背景
## Research Question and Background

解释研究对象、已有方法的不足、作者提出的假设和研究价值。

Explain the study subject, limitations of existing methods, the authors’ hypothesis, and the value of the study.

## 局限与风险
## Limitations and Risks

记录数据偏差、外部有效性、负样本、统计功效、批次效应、计算预算和未验证假设。

Record data bias, external validity, negative sampling, statistical power, batch effects, compute budget, and untested assumptions.

## 复现计划
## Reproduction Plan

给出最小可行复现、完整复现、所需资源、成功标准和预计风险。

Provide a minimal viable reproduction, full reproduction, required resources, success criteria, and expected risks.

## Implications for the Current Project
